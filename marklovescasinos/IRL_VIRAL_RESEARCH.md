# How to make Mark's IRL clips go viral (research, Nov 5 2026)

## The key insight
Mark has ~2.7k Kick followers → almost nobody searches for or recognises him. Searches for small IRL streamers' Shorts
return tens to low thousands of views; the ones that pop are about the MOMENT (strangers, a crazy Vegas thing), not the name.
→ Our clips must work for someone who has never heard of Mark. Lead with the situation, credit him in caption/overlay.

## What the algorithms reward (2026)
- YouTube Shorts: % viewed + loops (short clips can pass 100% viewed through replays) and swipe-away in the first seconds.
- Instagram Reels: watch time, then sends per reach (shares in DMs = best for reaching non-followers), then likes per reach
  (Mosseri). Original edits are favoured over aggregators/reposts.
- Our own data: Context Loading Shorts get 1–2k test views; whether they get pushed depends on stayed % (we hit 61–70%) and avg % viewed.

## Moments to hunt for in the IRL footage (ranked)
1. Stranger interactions: funny exchanges, compliments, people recognising him, wholesome moments (street-interview energy = highest-ceiling format we measured).
2. "Only in Vegas/Nashville" spectacle: Fremont Street performers, Halloween costumes, wild sights. Viewers send these to friends.
3. Genuine reactions: hype, laughter, shock (positive only, no mocking Mark).
4. Mini-stories with payoff: setup → twist in 20–45 s.
Skip: dead air, walking with no event, anything sexual/violent/drunk-messy, anything making Mark look bad, gambling on YT.

## Edit recipe (fits every rule: no AI voice, no exaggeration)
- Frame 1 = the most interesting visual + a real quote caption from 0.0 s. No intro, no logo sting.
- Top text = honest situation line ("A stranger on Fremont Street asked Mark…"), not clickbait.
- Word-by-word captions, big, centred; key words yellow. Kick overlay always visible (bottom).
- Cut every pause > 0.4 s; punch-in zoom on the reaction; end on the payoff or a loop back to frame 1.
- Optional quiet music bed, ducked well under speech.
- Lengths: S 12–25 s (loops, YT + IG), M 30–45 s (stories), L 60–90 s (best full story; Kick-IRL audiences like context).
- Captions/titles ask a real question → comments (we need ≥0.2% engagement): "would you have done this?"

## Distribution
- YT (IRL only) on @WhyNotTop-3; IG Reels on a dedicated fan page. Different cut per platform.
- Post 2/day per platform at US times; reply to comments in the first hour; never delete.
